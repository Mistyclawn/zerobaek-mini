# 049 - Orbital Habitat Sustainability
## Description
Players manage an enclosed, self-sustaining habitat in Earth's orbit. The primary challenges include balancing resource consumption, dealing with micro-meteoroid strikes, and maintaining complex life support (hydroponics, oxygen recycling).
## Dependencies
- Graphics: Procedural generation of stellar backgrounds, low-gravity object interaction physics.
- Logic: Closed-loop resource management (air, water, nutrient cycling), modular structural damage simulation, and crew psychology modeling.

# 051 - Magical Bakery Tycoon
## Description
Players manage a magical bakery where the primary ingredients are exotic magical components and rare flora. They must track magical recipes, balance their inventory of volatile potions, and satisfy the whims of demanding magical patrons while maintaining their reputation within the local Arcane Guild.
## Dependencies
- Graphics: Cozy, highly decorated village/bakery environment. Visualizing potion effects (glow, smoke).
- Logic: Inventory management of magical resources (expiry, volatility, component interaction), simple quest/patron interaction system, recipe chaining and testing mechanics.
