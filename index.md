# 053 - Arcane Citadel Defender
## Description
Players are the headmaster/headmistress of a struggling magical academy. The game revolves around managing limited magical energy resources, recruiting unique student and faculty characters, and building defensive wards to repel seasonal magical threats from the surrounding wilderness.
## Dependencies
- Graphics: Stylized medieval fantasy environment, particle systems for magic effects, character portraits, and map/ward visualization.
- Logic: Resource management (mana, gold, focus), character skill tree progression, tactical battle system against tiered magic creatures, and time-gated narrative events.

# 049 - Orbital Habitat Sustainability
## Description
Players manage an enclosed, self-sustaining habitat in Earth's orbit. The primary challenges include balancing resource consumption, dealing with micro-meteoroid strikes, and maintaining complex life support (hydroponics, oxygen recycling).
## Dependencies
- Graphics: Procedural generation of stellar backgrounds, low-gravity object interaction physics.
- Logic: Closed-loop resource management (air, water, nutrient cycling), modular structural damage simulation, and crew psychology modeling.
