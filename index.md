# 048 - Pre-Collapse Ecosystem Rebuild
## Description
Players manage a small community trying to rebuild a civilization after a massive climate event. The focus is on sustainable farming, scavenging resources from overgrown ruins, and managing social tensions between different survivalist factions.
## Dependencies
- Graphics: Overgrown urban environments, procedural flora/fauna generation, resource icons (food, wood, scrap), and map visualization of resource depletion/growth.
- Logic: Inter-resource dependency loop (e.g., wood for tools, tools for harvest), faction reputation tracking, and procedural threat encounters (wild animals, resource raids).
